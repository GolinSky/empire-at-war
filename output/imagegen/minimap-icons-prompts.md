# Minimap icon generation

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. The four selected originals were copied unchanged to `Assets/Art/Textures/Ui/Icons/MiniMap/`. Unity imports their returned alpha channels as tintable sprites.

## ship

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Unity minimap monochrome sprite mask. Draw exactly one flat pure white #FFFFFF icon on an exactly uniform pure black #000000 square background. Subject: a simple top-down spaceship pointed up, triangular pointed nose, swept broad hull, short squared stern with twin engine shoulders. SOLID SILHOUETTE with no interior details. Crisp smooth vector-style hard edges, simple bold geometric shape readable at 20px. Centered at exactly square center, overall icon occupying 84% of canvas with equal margins. White ink only. Absolutely no checkerboard, alpha background, gray, transparency, textures, distress, speckles, gradients, shading, small details, noise, text or shadows. The BLACK background is intentional and must be flat pure black throughout; it will be converted to transparency by the game engine.
