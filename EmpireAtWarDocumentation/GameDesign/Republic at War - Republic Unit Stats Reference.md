## Fighters / bombers

- A-6 Interceptor — HP 60 | Shield 20 | Speed very fast | Ability: Hunt | weapons: 3× laser cannon | no targetable hardpoints.
- V-19 Torrent — HP 70 | Shield 30 | Speed: — | Hunt | 2× laser cannon | no targetable hardpoints.
- NTB-630 — HP 60 | Shield 30 | Speed: faster than Y-Wing | Ion Shot | Laser + Proton Torpedo.
- Y-Wing — HP 60 | Shield 30 | slightly slower than NTB | Ion Shot, passive astromech repair | 2× Laser + Proton Torpedo Launcher.
- Clone Z-95 — HP 80 | Shield 30 | fast | Proton Torpedo | 2× Laser, torpedo special.
- ARC-170 — HP 105 | Shield 35 | heavy fighter; S-foils boost speed | Lock S-foils, Astromech Repair | 2× heavy laser + rear laser turret + proton torpedo launcher.
- For fighters, these are weapon systems rather than the large ships' destructible/targetable hardpoints.

## Corvettes / frigates


### C-70 Corvette Refit

- HP 750 | Shield 600
- Speed: moderately fast, Power to Engines
- HPs: 6× Laser Cannon
- Types: weapon/laser only.

### C-65 Assault Refit

- HP 750 | Shield 600
- Speed: engine boost
- Ability: Power to Engines
- HPs: 6× Laser Cannon, 2× Concussion Missile
- Types: laser + missile.

### Carrack Cruiser

- HP 1,600 | Shield 1,000
- Ability: Power to Weapons
- HPs: 12× Dual Laser Cannon
- Pure anti-fighter weapon layout.

### Pelta Frigate

- HP 2,200 | Shield 1,000
- Abilities: Power to Weapons, Defensive Mode
- Defensive Mode trades movement speed for extreme shield regeneration.
- HPs: 4× Turbolaser, 4× Laser Cannon, 2× Ion Cannon
- These weapon hardpoints are effectively untargetable according to the wiki.

- Types: subsystem + laser/turbolaser + torpedo.

### Arquitens Light Cruiser

## Cruisers

- HP 4,700 | Shield 2,000

- HPs:

## Capital ships


### Venator

- HP 5,000 | Shield 3,000
- Ability: Power to Weapons / Fire All Batteries
- Hardpoints:
- Shield Generator
- Engines
- Hangar
- SPHA emplacement — normally inoperable
- 8× Heavy Turbolaser
- 2× Medium Turbolaser
- 4× Laser Cannon in the infobox; overview describes these as 2 normal + 2 point-defense batteries
- 1× Proton Torpedo launcher — fires a 4-torpedo salvo
- Carries up to 8 fighter + 5 bomber squadrons in reserves.

### Pride of the Core — Mandator II

- HP 9,000 | Shield 8,000
- Abilities: Tractor Beam, Power to Weapons / Fire All Batteries
- Hardpoints:
- 2× Shield Generator
- 3× Engines
- 2× Hangar
- 1× Tractor Beam

- 4× Heavy Turbolaser
- 8× Twin Turbolaser
- 8× Ion Cannon
- 16× Point-Defense Laser
- 8× Concussion Missile
- 4× Proton Torpedo
- This is by far the largest Republic skirmish ship.

## Hardpoint types used by the Republic

- Weapon: Laser, Point Defense Laser, Turbolaser, Ion, Concussion Missile, Proton Torpedo, SPHA/beam.
- Defense: Shield Generator.
- Mobility: Engines.
- Carrier: Hangar.
- Control/special: Tractor Beam and special-weapon hardpoints.
