# Rebel & New Republic Fleet — Ship Balance

## Balance Rules

All stats are proposed for the Unity RTS project, based on existing ship balance.

- **Speed:** Game units per second.
    
- **Price:** Base credits.
    
- **Build Time:** Seconds.
    
- **Hardpoints:** Count represents weapon mounts, not individual barrels.
    
- **Weapons:** Simplified from Star Wars lore and Empire at War mods.
    
- **Abilities:** Custom gameplay suggestions, not necessarily original EaW abilities.
    

### Ship Roles

- **Corvettes:** High speed, point defense, anti-squadron combat.
    
- **Frigates:** Escort, ion support, anti-capital torpedoes.
    
- **Cruisers:** Balanced ship-to-ship combat.
    
- **Heavy Cruisers:** Strong firepower and durability.
    
- **Carriers:** Squadron deployment and reinforcement.
    
- **Capital Ships:** Heavy weapons, shields, expensive construction.

# Point Defense System

## General Rules

Dedicated point-defense weapons should prioritize:

1. Bombers
    
2. Fighters
    
3. Interceptors
    

### Point Defense Characteristics

- Very high tracking speed.
    
- High accuracy against squadrons.
    
- Short range.
    
- Low hull damage against large ships.
    
- Automatic interception of nearby hostile squadrons.
    

## Weapon Target Priorities

### Point Defense Laser

- Primary: Bombers, fighters, interceptors.
    
- Weak against capital-ship hulls.
    

### Light / Medium Laser

- Primary: Squadrons and corvettes.
    
- Secondary: Frigates.
    

### Heavy Laser

- Primary: Corvettes and frigates.
    
- Secondary: Squadrons.
    

### Turbolaser

- Primary: Frigates and cruisers.
    
- Secondary: Capital ships.
    

### Heavy Turbolaser

- Primary: Capital ships.
    
- Secondary: Heavy capitals.
    

### Torpedoes

- Primary: Capital ships.
    
- Secondary: Subsystem hardpoints.
    

## Existing CR90 Corvette Changes

- 2x Point Defense Laser
    

This gives the CR90 dedicated anti-squadron coverage without replacing its existing weapons.
